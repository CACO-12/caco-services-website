About Property Checks & Reporting Property Checks & Reporting is available for discussion as part of our facility services offerings. What this service can include

We review the specific work requested and prepare a practical scope based on the property, workspace or operation. This may involve site preparation, agreed hands-on tasks, organizing supplies and materials, cleanup and communicating progress with the client.

For property checks & reporting, priorities may vary depending on the location, amount of work, access, safety procedures and whether the service is needed once or on a recurring schedule.

How we work

1. Request a quote

Share your location, the work needed, timing, size and any photos or site instructions.

2. Review the scope

We discuss equipment, supplies, access, scheduling and any specialist requirements.

3. Arrange service

Once the scope and availability are confirmed, we can discuss next steps.

Frequently asked questions

Can I request a one-time service? Yes, contact us with the details and preferred dates to discuss availability.

Do you serve communities outside Prince George? We welcome inquiries from Quesnel, Vanderhoof and Northern BC; travel arrangements depend on location and scope.

Are materials and equipment included? This is determined during quoting so you know what is included before booking.

Can the work be scheduled after business hours? Scheduling depends on the type of work, location and availability.

Specialist, hazardous, regulated or commercial transport work is subject to appropriate qualifications, permissions, vehicles and insurance; do not assume those are included.
