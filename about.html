ABOUT CACO SERVICES

Dependable support for properties, workplaces and projects across Northern BC.

CACO Services is a Prince George-based service business offering practical cleaning, general labour, warehouse support, transportation assistance, facility care and seasonal property services. We work with homeowners, property managers, businesses and project teams who need responsive help with clearly defined work.

Our Mission

To deliver reliable, safety-conscious and accessible property and operational support through clear communication, careful planning and work tailored to each client's needs.

Our Vision

To become a trusted local partner for homes, workplaces and organizations across Prince George and Northern British Columbia, known for dependable service and lasting client relationships.

Our Objectives

  • Respond promptly and communicate clearly about each request.
  • Provide realistic scopes, schedules and quotations.
  • Prioritize safe practices and respect for client property.
  • Offer flexible one-time and recurring support where available.
  • Build strong, professional relationships through consistent service.

How we work

We start by learning what needs to be done, where the work will take place and what resources or access arrangements are required. We then discuss a suitable scope, availability and pricing before confirming the job. Our service areas include Prince George, Quesnel, Vanderhoof and other Northern BC communities by request.

Our service approach

Our offerings span routine and detailed cleaning, site labour, inventory and warehouse assistance, loading and moving support, facility housekeeping, and outdoor seasonal care. We focus on practical, agreed tasks rather than promising services outside our capabilities or scope.

Qualified and screened staff

We value professionalism, safety and peace of mind. Our team includes certified staff and personnel with completed criminal record checks, helping clients feel confident when welcoming us into homes, workplaces and managed properties. Relevant qualifications and screening information can be discussed when arranging a service.
